# Phase 999.1 Plan 01 Summary

## Delivered

- A strict TypeScript collection model validates private visual references, review decisions, saved counts, duplicate totals, and `add` or `replace` reconciliation against the normalized authority catalog.
- A bounded offline OpenCV helper detects rectified tabletop cards or explicit binder grids and performs confidence-and-margin-gated ORB matching against user-supplied private references.
- The CLI confines all photos, references, reviews, reports, and collections to ignored `.local/collection/` storage and authority input to `.local/authority/`.
- Scan reports contain only hashes, canonical IDs, printing slugs, finish labels, confidence, geometry, counts, and reconciliation; they contain no photo bytes, paths, official artwork, or duplicated official card text.
- Human review can confirm a card or dismiss an empty/false detection. Reviews are bound to the exact photo hashes and detector result, and collection output is withheld until every detection is resolved.
- Synthetic tests cover tabletop contours, binder-grid duplicate recognition, uncertain exclusion, false-detection dismissal, stale-review rejection, authority identity validation, and exact reconciliation.

## Deliberate ceiling

The first slice requires local Python/OpenCV and private reviewer-confirmed reference images. OCR, acquired artwork, an LLM recognition path, and a browser review UI remain out of scope. Grid mode is the reliable binder-page path; automatic contours expect separated cards on a contrasting tabletop.
